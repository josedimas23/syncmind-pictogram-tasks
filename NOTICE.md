# Third-party assets

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the source code only. The images

## ARASAAC pictograms

Most pictograms in `images/` (fruits, school materials, fingers, photocopies,
menu options, "muy bien", etc.) are from ARASAAC.

> Pictographic symbols: Sergio Palao. Origin: ARASAAC (https://arasaac.org).
> License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
> Owner: Government of Aragón (Spain).

These pictograms may only be reused for non-commercial purposes, with the same
attribution and under the same license.
